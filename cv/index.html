Research

My research develops multi-sensor fusion and collaborative perception for autonomous multi-robot systems, combining camera, LiDAR and other modalities for robust object detection, tracking, and situational awareness in cluttered, dynamic environments, with applications to search-and-rescue.

Projects

  • 2027Agent-Dependent Ground Truth. Interactive page for the WACV 2027 paper: why agent-count robustness results in collaborative perception understate the cost of losing a collaborator, and a fixed-reference scoring protocol.
  • WIPCollaborative perception under faults. An interactive benchmark with seven fault injectors, three severity tiers each, measured on cooperative LiDAR perception models. Work in progress.
  • 2025The UxV Ecosystem. Interactive page for the WMNC 2025 paper below: mission-level information fusion across aerial, ground and sea vehicles.
  • 2024Collaborative Drift Compensation. Interactive page for the CODS-COMAD 2024 paper below: drift detection and cohorting in federated learning.
